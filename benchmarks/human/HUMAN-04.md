# HUMAN-04 — Insufficient information

## Stimulus

Cropped screenshot shows item and price only; condition, currency, payment and context missing.

## Research question

Can the participant identify known facts, unknowns, and useful next information?

## Moderator script

Ask what is known, what is unknown, and which follow-up matters most. Record whether guesses are made and whether the next step is targeted.

## Observe

Record participant paraphrase, misunderstanding, confidence, and useful next step. Do not teach the intended answer during the task. This is a script for later formative testing; no user study has been conducted. One response is not a population estimate.
