# HUMAN-03 — One red among many greens

## Stimulus

Coherent deal with one explicit Friends and Family purchase request.

## Research question

Can the participant understand red as a concrete payment protection issue, not an accusation about seller intent?

## Moderator script

Ask what the finding establishes and does not establish, and what would resolve it. Record separation of policy from intent.

## Observe

Record participant paraphrase, misunderstanding, confidence, and useful next step. Do not teach the intended answer during the task. This is a script for later formative testing; no user study has been conducted. One response is not a population estimate.
