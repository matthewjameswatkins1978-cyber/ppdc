# HUMAN-01 — Clean deal

## Stimulus

Mostly-green guitar purchase with item photos and Goods and Services.

## Research question

Can the participant explain that green means the known terms look coherent, not that safety or delivery is guaranteed?

## Moderator script

Ask for a paraphrase and one thing the result cannot guarantee. Record whether they distinguish coherence from certainty.

## Observe

Record participant paraphrase, misunderstanding, confidence, and useful next step. Do not teach the intended answer during the task. This is a script for later formative testing; no user study has been conducted. One response is not a population estimate.
