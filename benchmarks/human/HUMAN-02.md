# HUMAN-02 — Ambiguous bargain

## Stimulus

Low-priced phone; condition vague; Goods and Services available; battery health unknown.

## Research question

Can the participant understand why low price merits context without treating amber as a scam verdict?

## Moderator script

Ask what amber means, what evidence could reduce uncertainty, and whether dishonesty is proved. Record any accusation or useful clarification.

## Observe

Record participant paraphrase, misunderstanding, confidence, and useful next step. Do not teach the intended answer during the task. This is a script for later formative testing; no user study has been conducted. One response is not a population estimate.
