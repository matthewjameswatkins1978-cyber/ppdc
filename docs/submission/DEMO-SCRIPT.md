# PayPal Deal Checker — Demo Script

**Target runtime:** 2:35 (hard stop at 2:45; never aim for 2:59)

## Story

A buyer has a tempting second-hand guitar listing, but the repair history is incomplete. PPDC structures the evidence, asks a useful question, updates when the seller answers, and leaves the payment decision with the buyer.

## Run of show

### 0:00–0:12 — Hook

Show the app immediately.

**Narration:** “Buying second-hand means piecing together listings, messages, photos, delivery promises and payment requests. PayPal Deal Checker brings that evidence together before you pay.”

### 0:12–0:35 — Initial deal

Use the repaired-guitar scenario. If entering it manually, paste:

> Item: acoustic guitar. Model: Tanglewood Winterleaf TW4. Price: £155. Condition: used. Payment: PayPal Goods & Services. Delivery: tracked postage. Repair history unknown.

Point out the £155 price, used condition, tracked postage and unresolved repair history. The assessment is evidence-led; it does not declare the seller safe or fraudulent.

### 0:35–0:58 — Questions worth asking

Open **Questions worth asking** and show the repair-history question. If the live demo’s Copy action is used, verify the copied text before recording; do not expose personal clipboard content.

**Narration:** “Instead of declaring the deal safe or fraudulent, PPDC turns missing evidence into specific questions.”

### 0:58–1:18 — New evidence / What changed?

Add this to the same Deal:

> Seller says the headstock was professionally repaired after a fall and has been stable since.

Show the updated assessment and **What changed?**.

**Narration:** “The extra evidence makes the low price more explainable, but repair quality, current stability and value remain unverified.”

**Key line:** “A bargain should survive explanation.”

### 1:18–1:36 — Research and provenance

Briefly show **What I checked**. The current comparison evidence is insufficient for a reliable price verdict; present this restraint as the result. Do not claim market value or a confirmed bargain.

### 1:36–1:52 — Human decision

Show **Before you pay** and the explicit **Continue with PayPal Sandbox** action.

**Narration:** “AI can help explain the deal, but it cannot authorise payment. The buyer decides.”

### 1:52–2:12 — PayPal Sandbox

Show the transition into PayPal Sandbox using the Sandbox Personal buyer account. Never show a password, credential, autofill, or real card. The final recording may cut from approval to the PPDC return rather than show the login.

### 2:12–2:35 — Protection Passport

Show the verified completed Sandbox Passport. Highlight the completed transaction, £155, deal evidence at payment, material promises, findings, unknowns and provenance.

**Narration:** “After PayPal confirms completion, PPDC preserves the payment-time understanding: what was stated, what was checked, what remained unknown and which evidence was retained.”

### 2:35–2:42 — Close

Return to the tagline: **Check the deal before you pay.**

Optional: “Deal Checker understands and explains. The customer decides. PayPal moves the money.”

## Recording safety

- Wake Render and wait until the public app responds normally. Render Free may cold-start.
- Use synthetic demo data and the Sandbox Personal buyer account only.
- Disable notifications, close unrelated tabs, and keep dashboards and personal account details off-screen.
- Never show secrets, passwords, autofill, or a real card. Cut the Sandbox login if needed.
- Do not record the cold start. Keep the cursor steady and text readable.
- Do not publish the recording until Matthew approves the final video and its destination.

## Supporting scenarios

- **Primary:** repaired guitar; seller repair statement remains unverified.
- **Secondary:** conflicting console details and a Friends & Family request produce a concrete payment concern, with evidence sources shown.
- **Control:** an ordinary console listing stays restrained; a seller’s working-condition claim remains unverified and the app does not invent a price verdict.
