# Demo C — Pause on conflicting details
**Target: 35–45 seconds**

“This console is listed as a Digital Edition. Later, the seller calls it a Disc Edition, then asks us to switch to Friends & Family and decide quickly.

Those are separate issues. The model description conflicts, and Friends & Family is not for a purchase. We ask for a current photo and correction, and do not send purchase funds that way.

The evidence supports pausing until item and payment route are resolved. It does not establish intent, so we describe concrete problems without calling anyone a scammer.”
