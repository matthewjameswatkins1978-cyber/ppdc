# Demo B — New evidence explains a bargain
**Target: 40–50 seconds**

“This guitar is listed for £155 with a brief good-condition description. The exact variant and repair history are unclear, so the price is a question, not proof of a bargain.

We ask about a headstock repair. The seller says it was professionally repaired after a fall, then provides current close-up photos and confirms the variant. That makes the explanation more coherent: the repair could account for a lower price.

We still cannot verify repair quality, tuning stability, or value from messages. The assessment gets more specific but remains cautious.”
